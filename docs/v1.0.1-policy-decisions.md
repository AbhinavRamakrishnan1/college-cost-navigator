Qualifying payment evidence must be on/after consolidation disbursement, within the statutory window, and no later than assessment. A legacy boolean alone does not prove its date. Broad consumer summaries do not override these detailed regulatory conditions. No generalized permanent Parent PLUS/PSLF exclusion is introduced.
