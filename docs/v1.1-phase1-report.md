# v1.1 Phase 1 — Explainability, trust, and comprehension

Status date: September 19, 2026

## Scope completed

- Added expandable, keyboard-operable SAI and Pell calculation explanations driven by canonical engine results rather than a second calculation implementation.
- Added plain-language interpretation of SAI as an eligibility index and Pell as a Scheduled Award estimate.
- Added visible federal-aid-only scope disclosures that distinguish federal estimates from state, institutional, private, and school-specific aid.
- Added accessible inline definitions for aid, school-data, and repayment terminology, with links to the relevant methodology sections.
- Added policy-version context and a last-verified date to aid and repayment results.
- Added contextual next-step navigation from profile through aid, schools, comparison, and repayment.
- Added a local College Scorecard income-bracket suggestion for complete supported dependent-student profiles. The suggestion uses parent AGI plus student AGI, can be overridden, remains blank for incomplete profiles, and never converts missing Scorecard data to zero.
- Reflowed school comparison cards into a responsive grid for narrow screens.

## Calculation explainability

The SAI explanation consumes the same `SaiResult.worksheet` values returned by the Formula A engine. It shows applicable parent-income, allowance, asset, student-income, contribution, override, and final-bound rows. When the asset-reporting exemption applies, the explanation states that assets were not required; it does not manufacture entered asset values.

The Pell engine now returns descriptive trace metadata for its existing Maximum, Calculated, Minimum, ineligible, and statutory-special-rule outcomes. This metadata does not alter eligibility or award math. The UI identifies the path, preserves the actual SAI, separately shows the Pell-only `max(SAI, 0)` treatment for negative SAI, and explains nearest-$5 and unrounded-COA behavior when applicable. Incomplete and unsupported states remain fail-closed.

## College Scorecard bracket derivation

For dependent students, College Scorecard family-income reporting uses parent and student adjusted gross income. The app derives the suggested bracket locally as:

`parent AGI + student AGI → matching published College Scorecard income range`

The derivation runs only after the stored household produces a complete, supported aid calculation. Otherwise the bracket remains unselected until the user chooses one. The selected number remains a historical College Scorecard average, not a personalized net price or financial-aid offer.

Source: U.S. Department of Education, College Scorecard institution-data documentation, linked from `/methodology#scorecard`.

- Existing incomplete and independent-student profiles retain their prior fail-closed behavior.

## Warnings and limitations

- Automated accessibility checks cannot replace manual testing with multiple screen readers and assistive-technology/browser combinations.
