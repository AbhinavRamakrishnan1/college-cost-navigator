# v1.1 Phase 3 cost and non-loan funding foundation

Status date: September 21, 2026  
Scope: pure ephemeral cost projection and pre-loan funding-gap calculations only

## Scope completed

This chunk adds a pure in-memory funding domain for:

- explicit known, assumed, and unknown planning values with provenance;
- total-mode or component-mode annual educational costs;
- flat or user-assumed percentage cost projection for one through five years, with a four-year default;
- explicit annual cost overrides;
- Pell values passed from the existing aid calculation boundary;
- institutional grants, outside scholarships, other grants, and user-planned family cash;
- one-time, recurring, and custom-year funding schedules; and
- complete or assumption-required pre-loan funding-gap results.

It adds no UI, persistence, migration, network access, loan allocation, fee application, disbursement ledger, interest, graduation-debt calculation, comparison workspace, or repayment handoff.

## Architecture

The domain is isolated under `src/lib/funding/`:

- `types.ts`: planning values, cost inputs, funding schedules, traces, and result states;
- `schema.ts`: Zod runtime validation and cross-field invariants;
- `costs.ts`: annual cost resolution and nonrecursive multi-year projection;
- `nonLoanFunding.ts`: annual application of non-loan funding schedules;
- `plan.ts`: pre-loan gap orchestration;
- `index.ts`: public exports; and
- `funding.test.ts`: focused boundary, arithmetic, provenance, and architecture tests.

All public money values are integer cents. Percentage growth uses local `Decimal` operations and rounds only each final projected annual amount to whole cents using half-up monetary rounding. It does not alter global Decimal configuration.

## Planning-value semantics

- `known` requires a value and source provenance, with an optional as-of date.
- `assumed` requires a value, explanation, and assumption provenance.
- `unknown` requires a reason and carries no numeric substitute.

Unknown values are never converted to zero. Costs, grants, scholarships, Pell inputs, and family cash must be nonnegative integer cents.

## Cost projection

Annual cost input is an exclusive discriminated union:

- `total`: one explicit total educational cost; or
- `components`: tuition and required fees, housing and food, books and supplies, transportation, and other educational costs.

The schemas reject ambiguous total-plus-component inputs. Percentage projection always applies the explicit exponent to the original base amount—`base × (1 + growth)^exponent`—so displayed-cent rounding never compounds recursively. Annual overrides remain traceable planning values and do not alter later years' base-growth calculation.

## Non-loan funding and Pell boundary

The engine accepts, but never recalculates, Pell. Current-year Pell can be passed as a known value sourced to the existing verified aid result. Any later-year Pell must be separately known, explicitly assumed, or unknown. The module never projects Pell automatically and never reduces Pell because another grant or scholarship exists.

Institutional grants and scholarship renewal are never inferred. Family cash is a user-selected planning amount and is not derived from SAI; SAI remains an eligibility index, not a bill or required family payment.

## Gap behavior

For complete years:

`projected educational cost − grants − scholarships − family cash = signed pre-loan gap`

The result separately reports a positive remaining gap and excess non-loan funding. Excess is not silently discarded. No loan fills the gap.

If a required cost or applicable funding value is unknown, the year and overall plan return `requires_assumption`. When safe, the engine still exposes the known funding subtotal, known cost, and partial known gap while clearly withholding a definitive result.
