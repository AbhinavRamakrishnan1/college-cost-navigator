# v1.1 Phase 3 federal loan allocation and ledger

Status date: September 21, 2026  
Scope: pure federal loan elections, origination fees, dated disbursement ledger, and net-proceeds funding accounting only

## Scope completed

This chunk adds pure, ephemeral planning support for explicit Direct Subsidized, Direct Unsubsidized, and Parent PLUS elections. It enforces the approved annual, aggregate, lifetime, transition, current-year-usage, and institutional-allocation policy states without duplicating federal constants. It adds no persistence, migration, UI, interest, capitalization, debt-at-graduation result, repayment handoff, comparison, or private-loan behavior.

## Allocation architecture

The additive modules under `src/lib/funding/` are:

- `loanTypes.ts`: elections, institutional allocations, schedules, ledger records, and result-state types;
- `loanSchema.ts`: runtime validation and election/schedule reconciliation;
- `ledger.ts`: effective-date fee selection, per-disbursement fee withholding, net proceeds, and rate-cohort references;
- `loanAllocation.ts`: canonical selector orchestration, binding-ceiling checks, and funding accounting; and
- `loanAllocation.test.ts`: focused federal-boundary, ledger, accounting, and purity regressions.

## Student Direct Loan elections

Student borrowing is never maximized automatically. The planner supplies separate gross-principal elections for Direct Subsidized and Direct Unsubsidized loans. The subsidized amount must carry either school-confirmed provenance or an explicit planning assumption; SAI does not determine it.

The allocation engine calls the approved policy selectors for the annual combined and subsidized ceilings, ordinary aggregate-countable outstanding-principal headroom, and lifetime headroom. It additionally accounts for confirmed current-academic-year usage. Missing usage or history returns `incomplete`; an election above a binding ceiling returns `exceeds_limit` with requested and allowed amounts. No requested amount is silently reduced.

## Parent PLUS ownership

Every Parent PLUS election has its own parent borrower identifier while retaining the common student beneficiary identifier. Multiple parents share the same annual and cumulative student-level usage and statutory headroom. Repayment does not replenish the supplied cumulative usage. Credit and transition determinations remain borrower-specific and use the canonical Parent PLUS selector. FAFSA contributor identity is not used as PLUS borrower identity.

## Institutional allocation

`none_confirmed` uses ordinary canonical federal ceilings. `unknown` fails closed. A `known_total_limit` without an explicit three-way allocation returns `requires_institutional_allocation`.

An explicit allocation supplies separate Direct Subsidized, Direct Unsubsidized, and Parent PLUS portions plus source provenance. The portions may not exceed the known shared total, and each election remains subject to the lower of its allocated portion and the independently selected federal ceilings. No priority order is invented.

## Fees, gross principal, and net proceeds

The ledger selects the approved fee policy using the earliest explicit disbursement date. The selected exact rational fee is applied to each dated gross disbursement with the approved fractional-cent truncation. Each loan and the total accounting preserve:

`gross principal - origination fee = net proceeds`

An unknown future fee window returns `requires_assumption`; the last known federal fee is not carried forward. A rate-cohort reference is attached when the existing federal rate selector recognizes the first-disbursement date. Unknown future rates remain explicitly unknown and are not guessed. No interest is calculated.

## Dated disbursement ledger

Each loan record preserves borrower ID and role, student beneficiary ID, loan type, academic year, first-disbursement date, fee-policy version, rate-cohort reference when known, gross principal, fees, net proceeds, and every dated disbursement. Dates are never invented. The optional two-term helper operates only from explicitly supplied dates and records an assumption explanation and source.

## Funding accounting

The engine accepts the already-computed pre-loan funding gap and applies spendable net proceeds, not gross principal:

`pre-loan gap - net student proceeds - net Parent PLUS proceeds = signed remaining funding gap`

It separately reports gross borrowing, fees, net proceeds, remaining uncovered gap, and any surplus. Signed overfunding information is retained rather than silently clamped away.

## Result states

The public allocation path returns explicit `complete`, `incomplete`, `requires_assumption`, `requires_institutional_allocation`, `unsupported`, or `exceeds_limit` states with reasons. Missing history is never treated as zero.
