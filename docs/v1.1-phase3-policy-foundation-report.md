# v1.1 Phase 3 policy foundation

Status date: September 21, 2026  
Verdict: targeted Astra corrections implemented; ready for Astra re-review

## Astra correction pass

The targeted review corrections are incorporated without extending Phase 3 scope:

- Ordinary undergraduate aggregate limits now use aggregate-countable outstanding principal. Repayment of countable principal can restore this headroom; capitalized interest and other noncounting amounts are excluded. This is distinct from cumulative lifetime and Parent PLUS accounting, which repayment does not replenish.
- Higher dependent limits require a verified academic-year-specific qualification determination. A raw Parent PLUS denial, parent unwillingness, cap exhaustion, or a denial when another independently applying parent is approved does not automatically select the higher tier.
- Every public selector that produces a usable annual limit, aggregate headroom, lifetime headroom, or Parent PLUS result requires the same validated dependent-undergraduate/full-time/standard-year context and explicit institution/program identity.
- Academic-year start, enrollment-period start, and first-disbursement date are separate typed concepts. Direct policy uses the first, Parent PLUS uses the second, and fees use the third.
- Parent PLUS and lifetime transition determinations are bound to the requested institution, program, and enrollment period. Stale, expired, unresolved, mismatched, or internally incomplete determinations fail closed. Cancellation, withdrawal, and approved Title IV leave distinctions are explicit; contradictory withdrawal/leave facts are rejected.
- Lifetime history is a discriminated complete/incomplete model. A complete history requires every category amount and confirmed consolidation attribution; incomplete history fails closed.
- A known institutional limit is represented as a shared annual total across Direct and PLUS. It returns `requires_institutional_allocation`; no independent usable amounts or allocation are fabricated.
- Schemas now restrict date-basis values, reject reversed effective windows and open-ended fee windows, and reject Parent PLUS annual usage above cumulative aggregate usage.

## Supported and unsupported scope

The foundation supports dependent undergraduates, full-time enrollment, standard academic years, and an initial four-year planning horizon. Year four uses the third-year-and-beyond policy band, but callers must supply the actual grade level rather than infer it from a calendar index.

Independent students, graduate/professional students, part-time and nonstandard enrollment, transfer-specific determinations, shortened/nonterm programs, unresolved transition status, and unknown institutional program-level limits fail closed. A known institutional cap can be represented explicitly. User assumptions are not stored as federal policy.

Origination eligibility remains completely separate from repayment eligibility. No repayment selector grants permission to originate a loan, and no repayment formula changed.

## Selector architecture and fail-closed decisions

`originationSchemas.ts` validates every imported dataset at module load. Runtime input schemas separately validate scope, grade level, qualification state, prior counted principal, lifetime applicability, Parent PLUS facts, and fee loan type/date. `selectors.ts` re-exports the new origination layer so later code has one policy entry point.

Key boundaries:

- Higher dependent limits require a documented qualifying school determination or Parent PLUS credit denial.
- Parent refusal and Parent PLUS cap exhaustion retain the normal dependent limit.
- Ordinary undergraduate aggregate headroom uses aggregate-countable outstanding principal; cumulative borrowing and an undifferentiated servicer balance are not substitutes. Lifetime and Parent PLUS remain cumulative.
- Repayment, forgiveness, cancellation, or discharge does not replenish the new cumulative limits.
- All parents share one annual and aggregate Parent PLUS cap for a student, while a parent's transition exception must be supported by a qualifying loan to the student or that current parent borrower.
- Missing or stale transition, qualification, credit, cap, institutional-limit, or lifetime-history facts remain missing.
- A future fee window is unavailable; the current rate is not extended beyond September 30, 2027.
- Fee arithmetic uses exact integer ratios and truncates fractional cents. Fee gross-up and allocation are not implemented.
