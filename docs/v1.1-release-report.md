# College Cost & Aid Navigator v1.1 release report

Status date: September 23, 2026

## Release scope

v1.1 adds calculation explainability, guided profile setup, FAFSA contributor guidance, local what-if aid exploration, privacy-conscious print summaries, four-/five-year school funding plans, year-by-year federal loan allocation, origination fees and net proceeds, in-school interest, graduation-debt estimates, factual school comparison, and final decision-season tools.

The final product pass adds the About the Builder page, Aid Offer Decoder, Decision Season Checklist, adapter-derived plan completeness, a mobile-friendly funding timeline, provenance badges, calculation disclosures, one ephemeral baseline-versus-what-if funding scenario, polished student-versus-parent debt presentation, print/save wording, and a limited repayment handoff.

## Privacy architecture

Household profiles, saved schools, and explicitly saved repayment scenarios use browser-local IndexedDB. Funding plans, their one temporary what-if scenario, completeness presentation, and checklist remain React-memory-only and clear on reload. They are not written to IndexedDB, localStorage, sessionStorage, URLs, analytics, logs, or external requests. The College Scorecard dataset is a versioned same-origin static snapshot.

## Federal-policy scope

The aid engine remains dependent-student Formula A for award year 2026–27. Independent students fail closed. Origination planning is limited to the reviewed supported undergraduate scope and explicit borrower/history/institution facts. Unknown future Pell, rates, and origination-fee windows require a labeled assumption or remain incomplete. Federal policy JSON and approved formulas were not changed for this release pass.

## Funding planner capabilities

The planner supports four- and five-year horizons, published or user-entered school costs, grants and scholarships, family cash, student Direct Loan elections, Parent PLUS elections, origination fees, net proceeds, remaining gaps, projected surplus, in-school interest, and student/parent debt at graduation. It displays requested, allowed, applied, and binding-reason values without ranking schools or scenarios.

The baseline-versus-what-if feature duplicates the current plan once in memory and independently invokes the same planner adapter. It shows factual dollar differences only. Keeping or discarding the scenario never writes planner state to storage.

## Repayment handoff boundary

The funding planner links to the existing repayment route without transferring balances or financial values. It does not combine loan cohorts, merge Parent PLUS with student debt, invent consolidation, create a blended APR, capitalize graduation interest, or automatically run an income-driven plan on a mixed portfolio. Users must enter and confirm each supported repayment scenario.

## Accessibility

The final UI uses semantic headings and regions, labeled controls, fieldsets, keyboard-operable checklist and disclosure controls, visible focus, explicit non-color status text, step/year error recovery, narrow-screen reflow, print styles, and reduced-motion behavior. Automated axe coverage is part of Playwright; this is not a claim of manual screen-reader certification.

## Known limitations

- Estimates are planning tools, not financial-aid offers or servicer quotes.
- Scorecard values are historical public institutional data, not personalized prices.
- Future costs, Pell, rates, fees, and institutional aid can change.
- Institutional aid policies and renewable-award terms vary by school.
- Repayment results depend on verified borrower, loan, cohort, and history facts.
- The planner excludes private loans and does not recommend or rank colleges.
- Graduation debt is informational principal plus accrued in-school interest; it is not automatically a repayment-entry balance and capitalization is not applied.
- Funding plans, checklists, and what-if scenarios are not persisted.
